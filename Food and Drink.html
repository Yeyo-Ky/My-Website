Home Food and Drink Things to Do

Breakfast Enjoy some delicious quesitos, made from puff pastry and sweet cream cheese. Wash it all down with some cafe con leche.

Lunch Enjoy a fried favorite like alcapurritas with assorted meats. Try mofongo, a mashed side dish made with plantains. Or take it up a notch with pastelon, a beef lasagna with plantains. Dinner & Dessert Looking to try an island classic? Chow down on some arroz con gandules. Then wash it down with a refreshing pina colada, from where it was first invented! Need to beat the heat, try the famous frozen coconut in a cup treat, limber de coco.
