Due to it’s proximity to the equator, Puerto Rico is hot and humid. Consider things like heat and humidity sensitivity before venturing here. Puerto Rico has very temperate weather and most importantly, hurricane season. I’d recommend planning a visit between late June to mid July. Late August is also fine, but you could miss out on some seasonal events.

Puerto Rico has a sizable verity of hotels and alternative lodging. Hotels offer a high end living space with housekeeping service and other amenities. Recommended: Holiday Inn El Caney, Hyatt Bayamon, However, an air bnb can provide a more local an authentic experience. I recommend the Enchanted Sunset West Indies air bnb location. They offer 3 story lodging with separate rooms, a bathroom on each floor and two showers.

Something I’ve learned more recently is how difficult driving in Puerto Rico can be. First and foremost, Puerto Rico has a lot of bumpy and uneven roads, so I’d recommend acquiring a vehicle with decent space from bumper to ground. Secondly, Puerto Rico has many unmarked two way roads, often with sudden curves and turns. Please drive carefully and closer to the side opposite of the driver side. A lot of highways in Puerto Rico have tolls. So if you don’t feel like navigating a bunch of alternate routes, consider setting up an Autoexpreso system. You can purchase a transponder at select Total and Puma gas stations.
